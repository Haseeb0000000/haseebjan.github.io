Travel thoughtfully

Leave room for the mountains

An adaptable plan is part of the experience.

Think beyond a checklist

Roads, weather, permissions, and local restrictions can change the plan. Discuss alternatives and leave contingency time around onward travel.

Choose a pace that works

More places can mean more driving and hotel changes. Ask whether fewer bases, extra rest days, or a private route suit your group better.

Prepare for your own needs

Request a date-specific packing list. Discuss accessibility and dietary arrangements directly. Seek professional advice about altitude or exertion where relevant; a sample itinerary cannot assess individual suitability.
