# Heritage experience research and implementation

Researched 3 November 2026. Original Holiday Host wording and route proposals; no operator descriptions, photographs or customer reviews have been copied.

## What others do

- INTACH Kashmir presents five themed Srinagar walks, distinguishing the medieval old city, Mughal-era city and other historical areas. Its useful principle is to focus a walk around a coherent place or theme rather than treating all heritage as one sightseeing list. Source: https://heritageinkashmir.org/SgrHeritageWalks.aspx
- Kashmir Tour Packages Taxi presents a short old-city product alongside a detailed Jamia Masjid visitor guide. Useful presentation features include practical questions, neighbourhood context and a direct enquiry route. Their published fees, access claims and credentials are not adopted by Holiday Host. Source: https://www.kashmirtourpackagestaxi.com/jamia-masjid-srinagar-visitor-guide/
- Sunshine Tours and Travels publishes a four-day historical-tour outline that spreads Srinagar religious and heritage sites across a stay. This demonstrates an existing longer cultural-stay format; it does not validate supplier costs, timing or partnerships for this website. Source: https://sunshinetoursandtravels.com/upload/tour/itenary/itenary-f23f94adaf0520bb86b84cbf60401e07.pdf

## Our original implementation

1. A focused old-city walk proposal: Khanqah-e-Moula, Zaina Kadal / Maharaj Gunj and Jamia Masjid / Nowhatta, with the direction, stops and vehicle links adjusted by the local guide.
2. A separate SPS Museum and one appointed craft visit. Carpet weaving, pashmina, kani, sozni, papier-mâché, copperware, walnut carving and khatamband are choices, not a promise of visits to every workshop in one day.
3. A separate sacred-sites day with selected Hazratbal and Hari Parbat-area options. Shankaracharya is an alternative with steps and separate approach requirements; religious interiors and ceremonies are not guaranteed visitor access.
4. A four-day / three-night Srinagar heritage stay. No unverified guide price, named host, reserved hotel or partnership claim.
5. Links from navigation, homepage, experiences, stories and relevant Kashmir itineraries; dedicated enquiry links retain the chosen interest and destination.

The proposed half-day duration is an editorial planning allowance, not a validated operating timetable. Operator validation is still required for every exact route, access arrangement, visitor eligibility, host and fee.
