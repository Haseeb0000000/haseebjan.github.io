All your questions ↗
How do I book a trip?
Choose a journey, request your preferred dates, and send an enquiry by WhatsApp or email. The team must confirm availability, the total price, and final terms in writing before any booking or payment.
Are these guide prices or confirmed offers?
Guide prices help you plan. Your quote confirms availability, the total and exactly what is included.
What is included?
Each trip has a proposed inclusion list and an exclusion list. Most overnight proposals cover stays, breakfast and dinner, and agreed route transport. Flights, insurance, lunches, and optional activities are generally separate. Only your accepted quote determines the actual package.
Can I join as a solo traveller?
You can request a place on a proposed group journey. Room sharing, group size, departure viability, hosting, and any single supplement must be confirmed first. Women-only journeys also depend on confirmed staffing and a viable departure.
