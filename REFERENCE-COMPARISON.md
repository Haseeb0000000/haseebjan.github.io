# Wildfloc public-page comparison — 3 November 2026

Inspected all 123 sitemap URLs plus the linked reviews page: 124 public pages. Pages initially lacking headings were revisited after navigation settled. This covers the public page inventory and rendered controls; it is not a test of authenticated administration or completed transactions.

## Completed functional additions

- Separate local-experience enquiry cards for Kashmir, Ladakh and Jammu.
- Vehicle/transfer and self-drive enquiry path without inventing fleet ownership or rental prices.
- A Ladakh motorcycle landing page linking the existing four riding proposals.
- Searchable original planning guides.

## Existing parity

## Deliberate differences and remaining requirements

- No competitor reviews, ratings, founder identity, fleet claims, guarantees, images or written articles are republished.
- Holiday Host has 26 regional proposals (23 comparable reference routes plus 3 Jammu proposals). Non-regional tours and international destinations are outside the agreed scope.
- Online payment, coupons, deposits and live seat inventory require confirmed products, supplier costs, payment-provider setup and approved commercial policies.
- Founder/video/social proof needs the owner’s own material and verified profile links.
- The reference has a much larger article library. Holiday Host currently provides original introductory planning notes; additional long-form regional guides would be new editorial content, not a missing booking function.
- Verified availability, final hotel/vehicle/service descriptions, business hours, office address and approved policies remain needed.
- No reference messages were sent and no payment or rental booking was completed.
