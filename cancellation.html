Cancellation & refunds

Clear terms. Fewer surprises.

Draft cancellation information · approval pending

Draft policy — business approval required. This page is a transparent placeholder for the owner’s final policy. It must be reviewed and completed before accepting bookings or payments.

No invented refund promises

Cancellation charges, refund eligibility, and processing times have not been supplied by the business. This website does not advertise a refund percentage or a free-cancellation period.

Before any payment

  • Request date-based cancellation charges and the deadline for submitting a cancellation.
  • Ask which hotel, transport, ticket, or permit charges are non-refundable.
  • Confirm rules for amendments, no-shows, early departures, and unused services.
  • Ask how weather, road closures, government restrictions, and supplier cancellations are handled.
  • Get the refund method, processing timeline, deductions, and escalation contact in writing.

How to raise a request

Contact the business through your confirmed booking channel with your booking reference and requested change. Do not assume the request has been accepted until the business acknowledges it in writing.

Owner to complete: final cancellation schedule, refund process, supplier exceptions, and the complaints contact.
