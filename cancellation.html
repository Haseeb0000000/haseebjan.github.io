Cancellation & refunds

Draft business policy · final terms require owner confirmation.

Draft policy · owner review

Draft planning policy prepared for owner review. These sample terms do not reserve services or collect payment. The accepted written quotation must state the final terms before any payment; applicable consumer rights remain unaffected.

A cost-based draft designed to make deductions understandable before you book.

How to cancel

Send a written request through your confirmed booking email or WhatsApp, including the booking reference, traveller names and requested cancellation date. Ask for acknowledgement. The effective timing and supplier deadlines must be recorded clearly.

Proposed refund calculation

For cancellation before departure, the proposed approach is to return the amount paid less documented, unavoidable supplier charges and services already delivered. The draft adds no separate agency cancellation fee. The written quote must disclose any supplier-specific non-refundable hotel, ticket, permit or transport charge before payment; deductions should not exceed the amount paid for the affected services.

Date-based supplier conditions

Hotels and operators may use different cancellation cutoffs, especially for peak dates or remote routes. Obtain the actual schedule with your quotation. This website does not promise a universal refund percentage or label every deposit non-refundable. Ask for a cost breakdown if a deduction is proposed.

Weather, closures or business cancellation

Where services cannot be provided, discuss a safe alternative, amendment or cancellation. State any cost difference before acceptance and refund the recoverable value of unprovided services after supplier reconciliation. A credit or alternate trip is an option to agree, not an automatic replacement for a refund.

No-shows, early departures and unused services

Amounts already committed to a supplier may not be recoverable. The team should still assess recoverable unused services and explain the outcome. Do not assume that every unused item can be refunded or that all remaining money must be forfeited.

Refund processing and disputes

Proposed service target: initiate an approved refund within seven business days after the amount and supplier reconciliation are agreed, normally to the original payer/method. Give a written expected date if reconciliation needs longer; bank or payment-provider clearing time may be additional. Provide the calculation and transaction reference. Applicable consumer rights and complaint remedies are not waived.
