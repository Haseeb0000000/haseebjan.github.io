## Flow improvements

- Explore by destination, eight travel collections and season; related itinerary links and all-trip search.
- Dedicated winter, women-only, honeymoon, group, private, trekking, motorcycle and heritage/faith collection pages.
- Catalogue and travel-date filters include winter and honeymoon alongside existing travel styles; combinations, empty results, reset, duration and price sorting remain supported.
- Travel dates uses one photo per itinerary with suggested date links carrying the trip and date into the enquiry. Custom dates remain available.
- Winter women-only, winter honeymoon and offbeat winter routes now have original day-by-day plans, realistic overnight locations, meal plans, seasonal dates and winter stay checks.
- Detail pages retain route navigation, day accordions, inclusions/exclusions, stays, packing, FAQs, linked genuine review summaries and a staged enquiry. No payment is taken.
- Three new Wikimedia Commons photos have author and CC BY-SA attribution. Their responsive variants stay below 300 KB each.
- Prices remain planning guides. Dates, hotels, staffing and services must be confirmed in the accepted quote; competitors’ availability is never evidence of our availability.
- Pages CMS can edit collection wording/photos, trip tags, dates, itinerary and stays without changing the layout.
