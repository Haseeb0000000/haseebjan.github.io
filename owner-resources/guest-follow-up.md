# Owner message drafts

These are editable drafts. No messages have been sent. Use them only with the relevant guest and their agreed contact preferences.

## First response

Thank you for contacting The Holiday Host. To shape a suitable proposal, please share your dates and flexibility, traveller count, approximate land budget per person, preferred room standard and two main interests. Would you prefer slower days with fewer hotel changes, or more sightseeing? You do not need to send identity or medical documents at this stage.

## Heritage visit clarification

Would you prefer an old-city shrine and market walk, a museum and craft visit, or a separate sacred-sites day? Please tell us how much walking you are comfortable with. We will check the guide, current access, host participation and visit fees before quoting. Workshop purchases are optional.

## Quote follow-up

Have you had time to look through the proposal? I can clarify the rooms, meals, route or optional costs. If another follow-up would help, please tell me a convenient time; otherwise I will leave the decision with you.

## Honest feedback after a completed trip

Thank you for travelling with The Holiday Host. If you would like to share your experience, we would appreciate an honest Google review: https://share.google/1Lvy3mV15JqHaQZlp . All feedback is welcome and there is no reward or discount for a review. Please avoid including private information. You can also contact us directly about anything we could improve.

## Photo permission — separate from a review

If you have a photograph you would like us to consider for the website, please tell us which image and whether we may publish it there. Please only share images you own and have permission from identifiable people to publish. Choosing not to share photos does not affect your service.
