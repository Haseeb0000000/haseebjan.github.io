# Photographs that help a guest choose

Capture or obtain publication permission before uploading. Never upload guest documents or private booking records to this public repository.

- Actual room category: daylight wide view, bedding, bathroom, heating and entrance / stairs.
- Vehicle: exterior, seats and luggage space; avoid publishing unnecessary registration or personal details.
- Team / guide: an introduction and portrait with consent; describe the role accurately.
- Heritage: exterior and architectural details where photography is allowed, without disturbing worship.
- Artisan: get agreement about the visit and publication before showing the person, workspace or work in progress. Do not imply a partnership that has not been agreed.
- Guest experience: use only photos with appropriate owner and subject permission; do not use a stock group as a real Holiday Host departure.

Use the Pages CMS upload control. The build creates responsive copies and removes image metadata. Write a specific description and keep destination inspiration separate from evidence of the actual booked service.
