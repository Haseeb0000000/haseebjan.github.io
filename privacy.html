What this website does

The enquiry form processes the details you enter in your browser to prepare an WhatsApp message, email draft, or copyable text. It does not submit your information to a website database or use browser storage. Sending the email shares those details with your email provider and the receiving business.

Information you may choose to share

Name, email, optional phone number, travel dates, destination, group size, trip length, and your preferences. Please do not include passport scans, payment card details, medical records, or other sensitive information in this enquiry.

Hosting and external services

The website is designed for GitHub Pages hosting. Hosting providers may process connection information such as IP addresses. Google Fonts requests expose connection information to Google. Email and WhatsApp links open third-party services under their own terms. No analytics, advertising scripts, or tracking cookies have been added by this redesign.

Business handling after you send

Owner details needed: legal data controller name, retention period, access controls, service providers, rights request procedure, and applicable contact address. These have not been supplied and are not represented as verified.

Questions

Use sales@theholidayhost.in to ask the business about its information handling before sharing additional personal details. The phone and WhatsApp number were supplied by the owner; the email was carried over from the original site.
