Booking terms

Draft business policy · final terms require owner confirmation.

Draft policy · owner review

Draft planning policy prepared for owner review. These sample terms do not reserve services or collect payment. The accepted written quotation must state the final terms before any payment; applicable consumer rights remain unaffected.

A practical draft for clear quotations, responsible planning and written confirmation.

Enquiry and quotation

An enquiry, guide price or suggested start date is a planning request, not a confirmed booking. Ask for a written quotation showing the legal business/payee name, traveller details, exact dates, named properties, room categories, meal plan, transport, services, exclusions, total price and taxes.

Guide prices and package basis

Displayed amounts are sample per-person shared-occupancy land-package guides. Flights, personal purchases, insurance and optional services are excluded unless listed. Hotel upgrades, single rooms, peak dates, children, permits and local transport can change the price. No unsupported discount, star classification or partner relationship is implied.

Proposed deposit and balance

Suggested payment schedule: 20% of the confirmed package total as a deposit after the quotation is accepted and suppliers can be secured; the remaining 80% seven days before departure. For bookings within seven days, full payment may be required. Any earlier supplier deadline must be disclosed and accepted before payment. This schedule applies only when adopted in your written quote.

Payee and confirmation

Use only the payee and payment method shown on the business invoice and independently confirmed with the team. Request a receipt and written booking confirmation. The website does not take card details or online payment. Never treat a chat message alone as proof that a hotel or ticket is reserved.

Accommodation and substitutions

Sample hotels and room descriptions are proposals. The final quote must identify the property, occupancy, amenities and check-in rules. A material substitution must be explained with a suitable alternative and any price difference for the traveller to accept; a named hotel is not guaranteed by a planning page.

Transport, access and activities

Vehicle category, luggage capacity, driver hours, kilometres, fuel, tolls, local union vehicles, permits and activity tickets must be itemised. Roads, Gondola operations, park access, pilgrimage services, riding and trekking stages depend on current conditions and permissions. No special access or wildlife, snowfall or blossom sighting is guaranteed.

Changes requested by travellers

Request amendments in writing. The team should disclose availability, supplier charges, additional cost or savings before the change is accepted. Name/date changes and unused services may follow supplier-specific rules disclosed before booking.

Group viability and operator changes

The quotation should state any minimum group requirement and the confirmation deadline. If the proposed departure cannot operate, discuss a revised date or comparable alternative; if the traveller declines, reconcile and refund recoverable unprovided services under the cancellation policy. Do not assume a future credit must be accepted.

Preparation and suitability

Travellers should check identification, permissions, personal insurance and activity eligibility. Discuss accessibility, room needs, dietary preferences and altitude or exertion suitability before booking. Seek individual professional advice where appropriate. Ladakh plans must allow at least 48 hours of rest on arrival before higher-altitude travel, following current official guidance.

Concerns and complaints

Raise concerns promptly with the trip coordinator so practical remedies can be considered. After travel, send the booking reference, facts, requested remedy and relevant receipts to the enquiry email. Request a written acknowledgement and resolution timetable. Nothing here excludes applicable statutory remedies.
