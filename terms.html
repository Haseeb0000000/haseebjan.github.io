Booking terms

Know the details before you commit.

Draft booking terms · final business terms pending

Draft policy — business approval required. This page is a transparent placeholder for the owner’s final policy. It must be reviewed and completed before accepting bookings or payments.

Enquiries are not bookings

Browsing a sample itinerary or preparing an enquiry does not reserve accommodation, transport, tickets, or any other service. The website does not accept payment.

Confirm the proposal in writing

Before booking, obtain the legal business name, invoice details, exact dates, guest names, accommodation and room categories, transport arrangements, meal plan, itemised inclusions and exclusions, total price, applicable taxes, and service availability.

Payment terms still needed

Owner to supply: deposit amount, balance deadline, accepted payment methods, verified payee, receipt process, and the point at which a booking becomes confirmed. No payment timetable or guarantee is assumed here.

Travel responsibilities

Ask about identification, permits, insurance, accessibility needs, altitude suitability, and restrictions relevant to your chosen route. Service-specific conditions must be disclosed in your final proposal.

Changes and disputes

Owner to supply: procedures for itinerary changes, unavailable services, disruption, complaints, governing terms, and dispute resolution. Review the draft cancellation page and request final conditions before paying.
