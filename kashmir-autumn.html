The journey

Your 7-day itinerary

7 days · Srinagar start
DAY01Srinagar · Nishat · Dal LakeLake light and a garden afternoonSettle into Srinagar before choosing one Mughal garden for the afternoon.+Toggle day details

Nishat and a Dal-side walk make a gentle first introduction without crossing the city repeatedly. If your arrival is early enough, request a shikara towards evening; a late flight simply means a quieter welcome and the lake visit moves to another day. Leave the old-city walk for its own day so there is time to enjoy it.

Places & stops

  • Nishat Bagh: terraces and chinar shade, subject to opening
  • Dal Lake boulevard: an easy waterfront stroll

Experiences

  • Optional evening shikara on Dal Lake
  • Kahwa after the journey, with time to settle in
OvernightSrinagarMealsDinner option
DAY02Pampore · PahalgamAcross the saffron countryTravel south through Pampore before continuing towards Pahalgam.+Toggle day details

Choose a pre-arranged grower conversation or the Awantipora heritage stop rather than treating every roadside stop as compulsory. A farm visit depends on a willing host; saffron flowers, apple picking and harvest activity cannot be promised for every autumn date. Reach Pahalgam with daylight left for a first walk beside the Lidder.

Places & stops

  • Pampore: cultivation stories with a participating grower
  • Awantipora: an optional heritage pause on the onward route
  • Lidder riverbank: an evening walk near the stay

Experiences

  • A seasonal grower visit arranged ahead
  • A local lunch requested separately, with dietary preferences discussed
OvernightPahalgamMealsBreakfast & dinner; hosted lunch optional
DAY03Aru · Betaab · PahalgamChoose your own Pahalgam paceGive the Pahalgam surroundings a full day.+Toggle day details

Choose Aru for village and meadow time, or combine a shorter Aru visit with Betaab if local transport and daylight allow. Travellers who prefer fewer transfers can stay longer in one valley and return for tea beside the river. Pony activities are optional, separately agreed and dependent on a suitable operator; walking is a complete experience in its own right.

Places & stops

  • Aru Valley, access permitting
  • Betaab Valley, if selected
  • Pahalgam riverside

Experiences

  • Valley walks at your own pace
  • Optional pony activity with terms agreed first
OvernightPahalgamMealsBreakfast & dinner
DAY04Pahalgam · Achabal · Kokernag · SrinagarAchabal and the southern springsLeave Pahalgam for a considered southern-valley return.+Toggle day details

Request Achabal and Kokernag as the main garden stops, with an early start and enough time for lunch before continuing to Srinagar. Verinag can replace a stop if the driver confirms a workable day. Daksum is better treated as an extra-night extension rather than another compulsory detour on this transfer.

Places & stops

  • Achabal: a spring-fed garden stop on the selected return route
  • Kokernag: garden paths and spring surroundings
  • Verinag: an alternative to one of the garden stops

Experiences

  • A slower garden walk between road stages
  • A lunch break planned before the return to Srinagar
OvernightSrinagarMealsBreakfast & dinner
DAY05Gulmarg · SrinagarMountain light in GulmargTake the Tangmarg approach to Gulmarg, allowing a tea break before the climb.+Toggle day details

At Gulmarg, choose a lower meadow walk or a ticketed Gondola outing, depending on weather and your interests. Gondola phases and local transfers are priced separately unless included in your package. Return with a sensible daylight buffer rather than adding another distant valley to the same day.

Places & stops

  • Tangmarg: a practical approach-road pause
  • Gulmarg: meadow or snow-appropriate lower paths

Experiences

  • A forest-edge walk matched to conditions
  • Gondola only when the phase and tickets have been confirmed
OvernightSrinagarMealsBreakfast & dinner
DAY06Old Srinagar · Nigeen or DalSrinagar through local eyesSpend the day close to Srinagar instead of adding another long drive.+Toggle day details

Request a locally guided walk around the Jhelum-side old city, allowing time for architecture, a bakery stop and a craft demonstration arranged with a willing artisan. Choose Nigeen-side quiet time or a Dal outing later. Dachigam may replace the city programme if permissions are confirmed; it is not automatically added to an already full day.

Places & stops

  • Jhelum-side old city: a guide-selected walking route
  • An artisan workspace: by appointment and permission
  • Nigeen or Dal: choose a relaxed lakeside finish

Experiences

  • A bakery and kahwa stop with dietary needs discussed
  • A craft demonstration without an obligation to shop
OvernightSrinagar; houseboat upgrade on requestMealsBreakfast & dinner
DAY07Srinagar departureOne more valley morningKeep your final morning flexible around your flight.+Toggle day details

A brief lakefront stop or time for shopping may fit before the arranged transfer. Confirm pickup time and allow a generous buffer for traffic and airport procedures.

Places & stops

  • Srinagar departure: arranged departure transfer

Experiences

  • A relaxed breakfast when departure timing allows
  • A short final stop only if the transfer buffer is protected

Departure timing takes priority. Extra stops are only considered after allowing a transfer buffer.

Before you decide

Your questions, answered.

Who is this trip for?

Couples, friends, families and solo travellers who enjoy exploring at a comfortable pace. Tell us who is travelling and we’ll help you choose the right format.

Where does the trip begin?

The journey starts in Srinagar. Share your arrival plans so pickup and the first day can fit around your travel.

Can I personalise the itinerary?

Yes—tell us which places matter most, your preferred pace and the kind of stay you enjoy. Ask about extra nights, a private trip or a different starting date.

How active is this trip?

The itinerary is rated variable. Tell us if you prefer shorter walks, more rest time or fewer hotel changes.

What if the weather changes?

Mountain weather and local access can affect the route. We’ll discuss suitable alternatives with you when planning your journey.

How do I book?

Choose your dates and send us an enquiry. We’ll share the available stay options, final package price and booking terms so you can decide.
