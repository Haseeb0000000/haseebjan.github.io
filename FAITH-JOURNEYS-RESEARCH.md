# Bait-ul-Meeras correction and faith journeys

Researched 3 November 2026. These are original route proposals, not confirmed departures or claims of permission from religious institutions.

## Museum correction

The owner intended **Bait-ul-Meeras**, the HELP Foundation heritage house and cultural centre in Aali Kadal, Srinagar. Website content, existing heritage itineraries, experience descriptions, the field note and enquiry wording now use this museum instead of SPS Museum. HELP Foundation describes its gallery, traditional crafts and manuscript resources; access to particular spaces or demonstrations still needs direct confirmation.

Primary source: https://www.helpfoundation.live/cultural-preservation

## Distinct routes

- Muslim shrine journey: 5 days / 4 nights, Srinagar base. Separate old-city, Hazratbal / Makhdoom Sahib and Charar-e-Sharief days. Aishmuqam is an extra southern-valley extension.
- Hindu temple journey: 5 days / 4 nights. Separate Srinagar hill-temple, Kheer Bhawani / Tulmulla and Mattan–Martand days. The living Mattan spring-temple area is distinguished from the Martand archaeological remains.
- Sikh gurdwara journey: 5 days / 4 nights. Srinagar Chatti Padshahi, Baramulla Chatti Padshahi and Mattan Sahib each have their own day. Pathar Sahib requires a separately planned Ladakh extension and arrival rest.
- Christian church and heritage stay: 4 days / 3 nights. Requested visits to Holy Family Catholic Church and All Saints Church, then a separate Bait-ul-Meeras day. St Joseph’s Church in Baramulla is an optional extra day; adjacent school and hospital facilities are excluded from tourism access.

Prices remain on request until named rooms, guides, vehicles and actual costs are verified. No ritual, ceremony, priority entry, festival date, religious accommodation or community meal is sold as an assumed package inclusion. Amarnath remains a separate seasonal yatra with official requirements, rather than part of the temple sightseeing proposal.
