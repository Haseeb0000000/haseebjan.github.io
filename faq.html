Travel FAQs

A little clarity before you go.

Useful answers for the first stages of planning. Always confirm trip-specific details in your written proposal.

Are these fixed packages?

No. These are clearly marked sample itineraries. Dates, hotels, transport, inclusions, and prices need to be agreed in a written quote.

How do I request a price?

Use the trip enquiry form to prepare a request, then send it through your email app. Share your dates, group size, preferred stays, and any budget preferences so the proposal can be specific.

Does the enquiry form send my request automatically?

No. It prepares a summary on your device. You must open and send the email draft or copy it into your email service. No enquiry is received until you send it successfully.

Can I contact the business on WhatsApp?

A WhatsApp number has not been verified, so WhatsApp messaging is currently disabled. Email or call using the contact details carried over from the original site, which also await owner reconfirmation.

Are flights, gondola tickets, and activities included?

Only items expressly listed in your final written quote should be treated as included. Ask about flights, entry fees, local union vehicles, permits, boating, and gondola tickets before booking.

What if weather or road closures affect my trip?

Routes and activities may change. Ask the business to set out alternate arrangements, extra costs, cancellation conditions, and contingency plans in writing before paying.

What should I know about Ladakh travel?

High-altitude routes need careful planning, suitable acclimatisation time, current permissions, and individual medical advice. The sample itinerary is not a medical assessment or a guarantee that a route is suitable for your group.

What are the payment and refund rules?

The business has not supplied final booking, payment, cancellation, or refund terms. Draft policy pages identify what needs confirmation. Request and agree the final terms in writing before any payment.
