Sample itinerary

The Ladakh Discovery

Wide horizons and a considered pace through the high mountains.

Duration8 days / 7 nights
RegionLadakh
StyleFlexible / tailor-made

The route: Leh · Nubra · Pangong

Suggested nights: 4 Leh · 2 Nubra · 1 Pangong

Planning example only. Hotels, meals, transfers, permits, entry tickets, and activities are not confirmed or included unless explicitly listed in your written quote.

Your journey, day by day.

Day 01 · Arrive & rest in Leh

Transfer to your stay and keep the day free for rest. High-altitude travel suitability and acclimatisation must be discussed with a qualified clinician before travel.

Suggested overnight: Leh

Day 02 · A second gentle day

Retain another easy day in Leh. Any short outing depends on how you feel and local advice; do not press on if unwell.

Suggested overnight: Leh

Day 03 · Around Leh

Consider a local cultural excursion after acclimatisation. Confirm monastery opening times, entry fees, and transport in your final plan.

Suggested overnight: Leh

Day 04 · Toward Nubra Valley

Travel to Nubra only when health, road conditions, and required permissions allow. The route may change.

Suggested overnight: Nubra

Day 05 · A day in Nubra

Make room for local exploration and rest. Optional experiences, local guides, and entry tickets are not assumed included.

Suggested overnight: Nubra

Day 06 · Journey to Pangong

Travel only on a confirmed, suitable route. A high-altitude overnight is optional and must be assessed for your group; an alternative stay may be required.

Suggested overnight: Pangong, subject to suitability

Day 07 · Return to Leh

Return to Leh with time to rest before departure. Keep this buffer flexible if roads or weather interrupt the circuit.

Suggested overnight: Leh

Day 08 · Departure from Leh

Transfer for your onward journey. Maintain contingency time outside this sample schedule for disruptions.

Suggested overnight: Departure

What to ask for in your quote

  • Named accommodation, room category, occupancy, and confirmed meal plan.
  • Vehicle type, pickup and drop-off points, driver charges, parking, and local transport restrictions.
  • Itemised activities, permits, entry fees, applicable taxes, and all optional costs.
  • Payment schedule, cancellation terms, and arrangements if the route changes.

Plan for flexibility

Weather, road access, local restrictions, and personal needs can alter any day. Keep onward arrangements flexible and discuss suitable contingency time.

Flights, insurance, personal expenses, and any service not named in your quote should be treated as excluded.
