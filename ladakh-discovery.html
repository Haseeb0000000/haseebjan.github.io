Know the details

What’s in the plan.

Proposed inclusions need supplier confirmation. Only your accepted written quote determines the actual package.

Proposed inclusions

  • Accommodation for the stated nights on the quoted sharing basis
  • Breakfast and dinner on overnight days; arrival dinner and departure breakfast
  • Trip-route vehicle, driver, fuel, tolls, and parking as itemised in the final quote
  • Transfers at the agreed starting and finishing points
  • Local coordination and the guided visits named in your proposal
  • Proposed route permits and environmental fees itemised in the quote
  • Named cultural sightseeing and entry tickets to be confirmed

Not assumed included

  • Flights, trains, and travel to the trip’s starting point
  • Lunches unless a specific day explicitly includes one
  • Travel insurance, personal expenses, tips, and shopping
  • Single-room supplements, extra nights, and optional activities
  • Any tax, permit, entry ticket, or local transport charge not explicitly itemised in your final quote

“All-inclusive” does not mean every possible expense. Meals, transport, permits, activities, taxes, and supplements will be itemised in your final quote.

Before you decide

Your questions, answered.

Are these guide prices or confirmed offers?

They are owner-authorised guide prices for planning, not live inventory or a promise of availability. The final quote must confirm dates, occupancy, transport, meals, activities, taxes, and supplements.

What is included?

Each trip has a proposed inclusion list and an exclusion list. Most overnight proposals cover stays, breakfast and dinner, and agreed route transport. Flights, insurance, lunches, and optional activities are generally separate. Only your accepted quote determines the actual package.

Do you have confirmed departures?

No fixed departures or seat counts have been supplied yet. Request a future date and the team will check whether a group departure or a private alternative can be arranged.

What are the cancellation terms?

Final business-approved payment, cancellation, and refund conditions are still required. The policy pages identify the missing terms. Agree the final conditions before paying.
