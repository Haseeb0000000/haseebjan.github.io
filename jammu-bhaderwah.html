Know the details

Included & excluded

What’s included

  • Accommodation for the itinerary nights on a shared-room basis
  • Breakfast and dinner on overnight days; arrival dinner and departure breakfast
  • Route transport with driver, fuel, tolls and parking
  • Arrival and departure transfers for the itinerary
  • Local coordination and the guided visits listed in your package
  • Proposed locally coordinated hill excursions

What’s extra

  • Flights, trains, and travel to the trip’s starting point
  • Lunches unless a specific day explicitly includes one
  • Travel insurance, personal expenses, tips, and shopping
  • Single-room supplements, extra nights, and optional activities
  • Taxes, permits, entry tickets and local transport not listed in your package

Before you decide

Your questions, answered.

Who is this trip for?

Couples, friends, families and solo travellers who enjoy exploring at a comfortable pace. Tell us who is travelling and we’ll help you choose the right format.

Where does the trip begin?

The journey starts in Jammu. Share your arrival plans so pickup and the first day can fit around your travel.

Can I personalise the itinerary?

Yes—tell us which places matter most, your preferred pace and the kind of stay you enjoy. Ask about extra nights, a private trip or a different starting date.

How active is this trip?

The itinerary is rated moderate. Tell us if you prefer shorter walks, more rest time or fewer hotel changes.

What if the weather changes?

Mountain weather and local access can affect the route. We’ll discuss suitable alternatives with you when planning your journey.

How do I book?

Choose your dates and send us an enquiry. We’ll share the available stay options, final package price and booking terms so you can decide.
