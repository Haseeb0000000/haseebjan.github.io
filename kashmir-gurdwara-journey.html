Day rhythm Unhurried visit day

Current custodian permission, worship arrangements, weather and individual walking suitability decide access and the final sequence. No ceremony or religious interior is guaranteed.

Visit Chatti Padshahi in Srinagar near the Hari Parbat area, following the gurdwara’s current visitor guidance. If you wish to join langar or seva, ask the management what is appropriate and available; participation is not a prepaid package activity.

03A dedicated Baramulla gurdwara daySrinagar · Chatti Padshahi, Baramulla · Srinagar+

Day rhythm Dedicated road outing

Current custodian permission, worship arrangements, weather and individual walking suitability decide access and the final sequence. No ceremony or religious interior is guaranteed.

Give Baramulla its own return outing. The Chatti Padshahi gurdwara here is a different site from the Srinagar gurdwara of the same name. Confirm the meeting point and visit with local management before departure, and keep the road day focused rather than adding another distant district. Allow a planned meal break and return buffer.

Day rhythm Longer road day

Current custodian permission, worship arrangements, weather and individual walking suitability decide access and the final sequence. No ceremony or religious interior is guaranteed.

Travel south for a separate visit to Mattan Sahib, associated with Guru Nanak Dev Ji in Sikh tradition. Confirm the gurdwara’s current arrangements and leave space for reflection. Nearby temple and spring surroundings can be an optional heritage discussion for interested guests, with permission, rather than a compulsory multi-faith circuit.
