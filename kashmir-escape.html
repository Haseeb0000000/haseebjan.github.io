Sample itinerary

The Kashmir Escape

Lakeside mornings, alpine meadows, and time to simply take it all in.

Duration6 days / 5 nights
RegionKashmir
StyleFlexible / tailor-made

The route: Srinagar · Gulmarg · Pahalgam

Suggested nights: 3 Srinagar · 2 Pahalgam

Planning example only. Hotels, meals, transfers, permits, entry tickets, and activities are not confirmed or included unless explicitly listed in your written quote.

Your journey, day by day.

Day 01 · Arrive in Srinagar

Meet your arranged transfer, check in, and keep the afternoon relaxed. A lakeside walk is an optional introduction to the city.

Suggested overnight: Srinagar

Day 02 · Gardens & lake life

Explore Srinagar at a comfortable pace. Discuss a Mughal garden visit and an optional shikara ride; tickets and boating are only included if listed in your quote.

Suggested overnight: Srinagar

Day 03 · A day in Gulmarg

Plan a day excursion to Gulmarg before returning to Srinagar. A gondola ride requires separate availability checks and advance ticket confirmation.

Suggested overnight: Srinagar

Day 04 · Into the Lidder Valley

Travel to Pahalgam with unhurried stops as conditions allow. Settle in and enjoy an easy riverside afternoon.

Suggested overnight: Pahalgam

Day 05 · Pahalgam, at your pace

Keep a flexible day for local sightseeing or rest. Any local union vehicle, pony ride, or valley excursion must be separately specified and priced.

Suggested overnight: Pahalgam

Day 06 · The journey home

Return toward Srinagar for your onward journey. Departure timing and road allowances will be agreed around your confirmed flight.

Suggested overnight: Departure

What to ask for in your quote

  • Named accommodation, room category, occupancy, and confirmed meal plan.
  • Vehicle type, pickup and drop-off points, driver charges, parking, and local transport restrictions.
  • Itemised activities, permits, entry fees, applicable taxes, and all optional costs.
  • Payment schedule, cancellation terms, and arrangements if the route changes.

Plan for flexibility

Weather, road access, local restrictions, and personal needs can alter any day. Keep onward arrangements flexible and discuss suitable contingency time.

Flights, insurance, personal expenses, and any service not named in your quote should be treated as excluded.
