Plan with clarity

What a trip price really includes

The small print should be easy to understand.

Start with the basis

Guide prices are planning estimates per person on proposed shared occupancy, not confirmed offers. Check the room type, group size, dates, vehicle, and taxes.

Check the everyday costs

Breakfast and dinner feature in overnight proposals. Lunch, flights, insurance, extra nights, and optional activities are usually separate unless expressly included. Ask about permits, local transport, and tickets.

Keep the final version

Your accepted quote should list the total, inclusions, exclusions, supplements, payment schedule, and cancellation terms.
